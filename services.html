Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How are Apex engagements typically structured?

Most engagements follow our four-phase methodology: Diagnose, Design, Deliver, and Sustain. The duration varies—diagnostic assessments typically take 4–8 weeks; full transformation programs run 6–18 months. We tailor scope and team size to each client's specific needs and urgency.

What makes Apex different from other consulting firms?

Three things: practitioner depth (our partners have run the functions they advise), implementation accountability (we stay through execution, not just design), and our obsessive focus on measurable outcomes. We define success metrics upfront and track them throughout every engagement.

Do you work with mid-market companies or only global enterprises?

Both. While many of our marquee clients are Fortune 500 and FTSE 100 companies, we have a dedicated mid-market practice serving companies with revenues between $50M–$2B. We scale our team and fees accordingly—you get senior partner attention, not just junior analysts.

How do you price engagements?

We use a variety of commercial models including time-and-materials, fixed-fee, and value-based arrangements. For the right engagements, we'll consider outcomes-linked fees. We're transparent about pricing from the first conversation—no surprises.

How quickly can an engagement begin?

For urgent situations, we can mobilize a diagnostic team within 5–10 business days. For planned engagements, a 4–6 week lead time allows us to assemble the ideal team and conduct proper pre-engagement preparation.

Do you offer ongoing advisory relationships?

Yes. Many clients retain us on a long-term advisory basis after a major engagement concludes—providing senior partner access for board preparation, strategic decisions, and ongoing performance challenge. These retainers typically run 10–20 hours per month.
