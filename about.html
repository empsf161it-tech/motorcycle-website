Mission & Vision

Why Apex Exists

Our mission is simple: to help great organizations become extraordinary ones.

We were founded on the belief that clients deserve more than frameworks and slide decks. They deserve partners who think as hard about implementation as about ideation—who measure their success by client outcomes, not by hours billed.

Our vision is to be the world's most trusted management advisory firm, known not for our size but for the depth of our impact and the integrity of our relationships.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Our History

Two Decades of
Building the Extraordinary

1997

The Founding

Jonathan Webb and two colleagues launch Apex from a shared office in Manhattan with a simple promise: rigorous thinking, honest counsel, real results. First year revenue: $2.1M.

2003

First Global Expansion

Opened London and Singapore offices. Crossed $50M revenue milestone. Launched the Apex Leadership Institute for client capability building.

2009

Navigating the Crisis

Led 47 financial services turnarounds during the global financial crisis. Recognized in Financial Times as "the restructuring firm of the decade."

2015

Digital Practice Launch

Established the Apex Digital Transformation practice. Acquired DataEdge Analytics to strengthen AI and data science capabilities. Now 400+ digital specialists.

2020

Remote Resilience

Deployed 220 rapid-response crisis engagements globally. Developed the Apex Resilience Framework, now adopted by 300+ organizations worldwide.

2024

Today

1,400+ consultants. 28 offices. $3.2B+ in client value created. Ranked #1 in client satisfaction for management consulting by Forrester Research.
